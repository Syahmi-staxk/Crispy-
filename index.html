The Status HD preset is based on one sample PureStatus export: H.264 at approximately 3.4 Mbps, up to 1080 × 1920 and 30 FPS, with AAC audio. It does not guarantee identical results or prevent WhatsApp recompression. On supported browsers, video processing uses FFmpeg. On iPhone browsers without SharedArrayBuffer, ClearSend uses an experimental native recorder instead. Native processing plays the video in real time; keep this page open and your screen awake. Your media stays on your device. For this beta, use videos under 250 MB. Source location and other metadata are removed from exports. HDR video colors may need further work.
